At Soijen we play with a mix of illustration techniques to create fun and bright designs that celebrate outdoor adventure and wanderlust. Original artwork is created by co-illustrators/adventurers, Eduardo Donoso and Jess Young.

To give back to the wild places we love, we donate at least 1% of our sales to organizations dedicated to environmental conservation. We are proud to use recycled and responsibly sourced materials to create our products.
